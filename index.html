Everback privacy policy

Everback – Backup & File History · Privacy policy · Last updated 26 October 2026

Everback is a Windows app made by Jotech. It copies files from your PC to a drive or network folder that you choose, and keeps older versions of those files there so you can get them back.

What Everback does with your data

  • Everback reads the folders you choose to protect and writes copies of them to the drive or folder you choose. Those copies stay on that drive. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Everback keeps a catalogue of your files (names, sizes, dates, and where each copy is stored) on your PC and on the backup drive, so it can show you your files at any point in time.
  • Everback does not collect, send or receive any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no telemetry. It makes no network connections of its own; the only outbound links are the ones you open yourself to the Microsoft Store.

What is stored on the backup drive

The drive holds plain copies of your files in a folder named Everback, plus the catalogue and a log of backup runs. Anyone with access to that drive can read those files, exactly as they could read the originals on your PC. Keep the drive as safe as you keep your PC. Backups are not encrypted in version 1.

Licensing

The Microsoft Store manages your purchase and trial. Everback asks Windows for its licence state (trial, purchased, expired) through the Store's own interface on your PC. No purchase or account information is sent to Jotech.

Your choices

You decide which folders are protected and where the copies go, and you can change or remove them at any time in Settings. Deleting the Everback folder from the drive removes every copy. Uninstalling the app removes the catalogue and settings from your PC; it never touches the files on the backup drive or the originals.

Children

Everback is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If Everback ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
